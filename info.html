Discord Bots

I make Discord bots to enhance server experiences and automate tasks.
A good example of this is my moderation bot, Shield.
It's name stands for "Server Helper for Intelligent Logic & Event Defense"
It uses advanced tech to detect all banned words, users, and phrases.
It can detect slurs, swears, raids, and threats.
Shield will take action for you, muting users who violate the rules.
Just having it in your server adds way more security than you would with the builtin automod.
